## Good to know {#scope}

- The rules block by domain. They can't remove ads served from the same domain as the content, or the empty space ads leave behind.
- The privacy and unwanted categories can affect analytics, push, and updates. Choose what to include in the [builder](./Sub.md), and allow individual domains if something breaks.
- The rules are provided as is, without warranty for blocking results or false positives. Follow the laws where you live.
